## Announcements

The wiki's [2026 Family Photo](https://runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape:Family_Photo "RuneScape:Family Photo") is now live! Thanks to everyone who participated!

8 February 2026

Temporary accounts [have been rolled out](https://meta.runescape.wiki/w/Forum:Enabling_temporary_accounts "meta:Forum:Enabling temporary accounts") in place of IP editing.

8 April 2025

The RuneScape Wiki [has celebrated](https://runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape:Events_Team/20_Year_Anniversary_Party! "RuneScape:Events Team/20 Year Anniversary Party!") 20 years since it was founded!

13 March 2023

Advertisements are now enabled for logged-out users on desktop as a result of [on-wiki discussions](https://runescape.wiki/w/Forum:Funding_the_wikis "Forum:Funding the wikis"). If you spot a malicious ad, please report it on our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/runescapewiki).
